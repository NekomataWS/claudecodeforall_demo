Our Story

A cafe born from
stillness.

Mind Cafe was founded on a simple belief — that a great cup of coffee is more than a drink. It's a pause. A moment of clarity in the noise of the everyday.

We source single-origin beans from small farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, roasted in-house every week. Every cup is dialled in with intention — not routine.

Our space is deliberately dark, quiet, and unhurried. Come as you are. Stay as long as you need.
